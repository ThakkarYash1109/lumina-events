1. Data Collection

When you register or interact with Lumina, we collect the personal data you voluntarily provide (name, email, phone, organization, etc.). By submitting the registration form you give explicit consent for us to process this data for the purposes described below.

2. Registration Information

Registration captures contact details, role (host or vendor), and basic profile information required to create an account and enable communication on the Platform.

3. Booking & Transaction Data

For each booking we store event details (date, venue, services requested), agreed price, and the simulated escrow status. No real payment information is stored because the escrow system is a demonstration only.

4. Vendor Profile Data

Vendors provide business details, service descriptions, portfolio images, and licensing information. This data is displayed publicly on the Marketplace.

5. Purpose of Data Processing

Collected data is used to (a) facilitate matching of hosts and vendors, (b) enable messaging within the Platform, (c) operate the simulated escrow workflow, and (d) comply with legal obligations such as record‑keeping under Indian law.

6. Data Sharing (Internal & External)

Internally, data is shared between the host‑portal and vendor‑portal services to enable booking coordination. No data is sold or shared with marketing partners. If a third‑party integration is added in the future, this section will be updated accordingly.

7. Cookies & Analytics

The current implementation does not set tracking cookies or use analytics services. Should analytics be introduced later, a detailed description will be added here.

8. Third‑Party Service Providers

At present, no external services (e.g., payment gateways, email providers) store personal data. If such integrations are added, the specific providers and data handling practices will be disclosed.

9. Security Measures

All data at rest is encrypted using AES‑256. Access to the database is restricted to the backend server process. Transport security (HTTPS) is enforced when the site is deployed.

10. Data Retention

User accounts and associated booking records are retained for as long as the account remains active. Upon account deactivation, personal data is archived for a minimum of 12 months to satisfy legal record‑keeping, after which it is securely deleted.

11. Data Deletion & User Rights

You may request (a) access to your personal data, (b) correction of inaccurate information, or (c) deletion of your account. Requests should be sent to the contact address below; we will respond within 30 days in accordance with the DPDP Act 2023.

12. Children’s Privacy

The Platform is not intended for users under 18 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

13. Policy Updates

We may revise this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in law, technology, or business practice. The "Last Updated" date at the top indicates the most recent revision. Continued use of the Platform after changes constitutes acceptance.
