DPDP ACT 2023 COMPLIANCE

Privacy & Trust Charter

Last Updated: November 2026 | Effective for Lumina Luxury Marketplace Platform

1. Data Collection & Explicit Consent

Lumina collects host contact details, event schedule parameters, and vendor business verification documents strictly for contract execution and escrow disbursement. In adherence to the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, personal records are encrypted at rest and never commercialized to external lead-generation brokers.

2. Sandbox Escrow & Payment Isolation

Payment transactions operate under isolated sandbox testing or RBI-regulated escrow banking accounts. Card details, UPI VPA handles, and net-banking credentials are never stored on Lumina servers.

3. Ethical Interface Commitment (Zero Dark Patterns)

In line with Semester 5 E-Commerce ethical governance: Lumina guarantees zero drip pricing, no pre-checked commercial add-ons, transparent cancellation terms, and verified cryptographic review linkages.
